The visual reference is [Vera | AI EdTech LMS Landing Page by JetDesigns](https://dribbble.com/shots/27845171-Vera-AI-EdTech-LMS-Landing-Page), supplied by the user.

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool, using the reference screenshots as visual inputs:

Sky and academy originals were converted to WebP for delivery. The branch and bird retain the generated native PNG transparency. The academy image is synthetic and is not a photograph of a real customer.
