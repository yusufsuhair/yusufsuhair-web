# antislop audit 001: yusufsuhair.xyz

Date: 2026-10-09. Mode: after (audit only, nothing changed). Commit audited: `08abe12` on `master`, live at https://yusufsuhair.xyz.

How it was checked: read every component in `app/` and `components/`; ran the live site in headless Chrome at 1280 px and 375 px; tabbed through every control; checked text contrast with `contrast-check.py`; requested every outbound URL; timed the intro overlay and logged console errors.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Lock = LOW. Pick the numbers you want fixed; anything not picked stays as it is.

## What already holds up

- Every project, social and product link resolves (HTTP 200).
- No horizontal scroll at 375 px on Home or Services.
- All 58 tab stops on Home show a focus outline.
- The hero's social icons are 44 px, labelled, and have a focus ring.
- Projects show real screenshots of real work, the experience timeline has real employers and dates, and the Services CTAs open WhatsApp with a prefilled message.
- No fake testimonials, no template FAQ, no "Trusted by" logo bar.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

**1. "Let's talk" leads somewhere you can't talk.** R-26, C-2.
The navbar's main button scrolls to `#contact`, and that section's only link is "View Services". The section says "just say hi" but has no email, WhatsApp link or form.
Evidence: `components/Navbar.tsx:20`, `components/ContactWidget.tsx:22-35`.
Fix: put the email and WhatsApp links in the contact section (Services already uses `wa.me/601123709141`), or point "Let's talk" straight at WhatsApp.

**2. The terminal dialog traps keyboard users.** R-32, R-26.
Escape doesn't close it. Tab is captured for command completion, so focus can never leave the input, and typing `exit` is the only way out. It has no `role="dialog"` or `aria-modal`, and the input has no label. The red close dot (12 px) and the X button have no accessible name, and Tab can't reach either of them.
Evidence: keyboard pass; `components/TerminalModal.tsx:314-333, 378-394, 420`.
Fix:
- Close on Escape.
- Only run completion when the input has text, and let Tab move focus otherwise.
- Add `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, an input label, and `aria-label`s on both close buttons.
- Return focus to the trigger when it closes.

**3. Small text below WCAG AA contrast.** R-25.
| Text | Pair | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Footer copyright; experience location; skills "N packages installed" line | zinc-600 on #050505 / #0a0a0a | 2.6:1 |
| Hero role line (11 px); stats labels; project role labels (10 px); skill tabs; period chips; "Watch on YouTube" | zinc-500 on #050505 / #0f0f0f / #0c0c0c / #141414 | 3.8 to 4.2:1 |
| Services "Jump to" and the "01"/"02" numbers | zinc-400 on #f7f7f8 | 2.4:1 |
| Services "No ..." minus icons (non-text, needs 3:1) | zinc-400 on white | 2.6:1 |

Evidence: `app/page.tsx:46`, `components/ExperienceWidget.tsx:132-135`, `components/SkillsWidget.tsx:117,137`, `components/HomeWidget.tsx:135`, `components/StatsWidget.tsx:34`, `components/ProjectsWidget.tsx` (role line), `components/ServicesWidget.tsx:229,525,535`.
Fix: zinc-400 (7.5:1) for small text on the dark pages and zinc-500 on Services; or keep the colours and raise the size to 18 px or more.

**4. Touch targets under 44 px on phones.** R-03.
Measured at 375 px:
- **Home (22 controls):** the menu button (36×36), every project-card CTA link (16 px tall), the category filter chips (38 px), the skill tabs (42 px), "View Channel" and "Follow" (38 px), and the "YS." logo (30×28).
- **Services (16 controls):** "Book Session", "Book Class", "Get Quote" and "Get Started" (36 px), "Chat on WhatsApp" (38 px), "View full pricing details" (30 px), the jump links (17 px tall), and back-to-top (42 px).

Fix: a minimum height of 44 px (`min-h-11`) on these controls, keeping the visual size where it matters.

**5. The mobile menu is a bare, unlabelled hamburger.** R-03, R-32.
It's an icon-only 36 px button with no "Menu" label, no `aria-expanded` or `aria-controls`, and Escape doesn't close the open menu.
Evidence: `components/Navbar.tsx:85-106`.
Fix: a 44 px button labelled "Menu", `aria-expanded`, and close on Escape. With only two links and one CTA, they could also simply stay visible on mobile.

**6. Headline numbers with no source, and two versions of them.** R-17, R-36.
"60+ Apps Shipped" and "5M+ App Installs" appear in the stats strip, the hero paragraph, the Services header and the page metadata. The experience timeline says "50+ Android applications", the terminal says "50+ mobile apps", and only one app (Ronaldo, 1M+) links to its source.
Evidence: `components/StatsWidget.tsx:8-12`, `components/HomeWidget.tsx:173-174`, `components/ServicesWidget.tsx:511`, `components/ExperienceWidget.tsx:48`, `components/TerminalModal.tsx:69`.
Fix: pick one figure, use it everywhere, and link the evidence (your Google Play developer page); or drop the figures.

**7. Facts that contradict each other, and an employer that reads as Google.** R-38.
- **Your "Mobile Developer" role:** the timeline lists the company as "Google Play Store", which reads as employment at Google. The terminal calls the same role "Freelance".
- **YS Academy start date:** "January 2026" in the timeline, "Jun 2026" in the terminal.
- **Career start:** 2019 (August 2019 in the timeline, and the year the stats count from), but the terminal's `git log` says "started coding journey in 2020".

Evidence: `components/ExperienceWidget.tsx:29,42-43`, `components/TerminalModal.tsx:100,104,213`.
Fix: one source of truth; label the role "Independent developer (Google Play)".

**8. About 30 em dashes in visible copy.** R-02.
- **Date ranges:** all six on the experience timeline.
- **Project titles:** seven, including "Slipstream — Interactive F1 Field Guide", which I added on 2026-10-09 following the existing pattern.
- **Elsewhere:**
  - section subtitles (Projects, YouTube, Instagram, Experience);
  - Services descriptions;
  - the OpenGraph image alt and the 404 page title;
  - terminal output.

Evidence: `grep -rn "—" app components`.
Fix: a colon in titles ("LepakMamak: Malaysian multiplayer browser game"), "to" in date ranges ("April 2026 to present"), a comma or full stop elsewhere.

**9. A console error on every page, and analytics that records nothing.** R-35.
`<Analytics />` from `@vercel/analytics` requests `/_vercel/insights/script.js`. The site runs on Cloudflare Workers, so that path returns 404 on every page load.
Evidence: `app/layout.tsx:3,123`; the 404 is logged on every load at both phone and desktop widths.
Fix: remove it, or switch to Cloudflare Web Analytics.

**10. No written design direction.** R-37, R-31.
There's no `DESIGN.md`, so these choices have no written reasons:
- the theme split (dark Home, light Services);
- the palette;
- the three loaded fonts;
- the terminal motif.

Fix: you write a short `DESIGN.md` (identity, palette, type, mood, dials) and I format it. I won't invent the direction for you.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

**11. Default blue-purple glow.** R-01.
Two blurred orbs, one blue and one purple, sit fixed behind the whole Home page. A blue-to-purple wash covers the stats strip, the contact card glows blue-to-purple on hover, and the 404 page has red and blue orbs. Everything else on the site is monochrome, so the glow reads as the AI default rather than your identity.
Evidence: `app/page.tsx:24-27`, `components/StatsWidget.tsx:18`, `components/ContactWidget.tsx:16`, `app/not-found.tsx:12-15`.

**12. Glass on 15 elements.** R-10.
`backdrop-filter` is on:
- the navbar and the floating terminal button;
- all six experience cards and the contact card;
- back-to-top;
- the play overlays and the modal backdrop.

On the cards the background is opaque (#0a0a0a / #050505), so the blur draws nothing and still costs GPU.
Evidence: `components/ExperienceWidget.tsx:125`, `components/ContactWidget.tsx:18`, `components/TerminalModal.tsx:341`, `components/BackToTop.tsx:26`.
Fix: keep it on the navbar only.

**13. Motion with no stop: cursor glow and a status dot that marks nothing.** R-13, R-19, R-31.
A 480 px radial glow follows the cursor on a `requestAnimationFrame` loop that runs forever, even when the mouse is still. The terminal button's green dot pulses forever, and the terminal reports "● Online & Available", a live status nothing backs.
Evidence: `components/CursorFollower.tsx`, `components/TerminalModal.tsx:61,177,347`.

**14. No reduced-motion support anywhere.** R-19.
There are no `prefers-reduced-motion` or `useReducedMotion` checks. With reduced motion on, everything still animates:
- every section's fade-up on scroll;
- the self-typing H1;
- the intro overlay;
- the cursor glow;
- three blinking cursors.

Evidence: grep shows no reduced-motion checks; the overlay timing was the same with `reducedMotion: reduce`.
Fix: wrap the app in framer-motion's `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`, and skip the overlay and cursor glow under reduced motion.

**15. Wide-tracked uppercase micro-labels, and a "mono" that isn't mono.** R-06.
The default label style is 10–12 px uppercase with 0.14 to 0.2em tracking:
- the hero role line;
- the stats labels;
- the card roles;
- the footer;
- every Services heading.

`globals.css` maps `--font-mono` to Inter, so the 26 `font-mono` classes render Inter, while 3 places set JetBrains Mono inline. Raleway is loaded and never used.
Evidence: `app/globals.css:10`, `app/layout.tsx:11-14`.

**16. A skeleton mockup used as the "Web Development" product shot.** R-22.
A hand-built mini browser with grey bars and traffic lights stands in for a screenshot, while the portfolio already has real ones.
Evidence: `components/ServicesWidget.tsx:38-73`.

## Outside the rule set, but worth fixing

**22. The canonical URL points at a domain that doesn't resolve.**
`metadataBase`, `alternates.canonical`, `openGraph.url` and the author URL all use https://yusufsuhair.com, which returned no response when requested. Search engines are being told the real page lives on a dead domain.
Evidence: `app/layout.tsx:22,59,76,97`.
Fix: https://yusufsuhair.xyz.

**23. The 404 page never shows the real path.**
`not-found.tsx` is a server component, so `typeof window` is always undefined and the page always prints `cd /this-page`.
Evidence: `app/not-found.tsx:35`.

**24. Missing ARIA semantics.**
- **Pricing toggles:** the "View full pricing details" buttons have no `aria-expanded`.
- **Skill tabs:** they aren't marked up as tabs (`role="tab"`, `aria-selected`).

**25. Dead code.**
`AboutWidget.tsx`, `GithubWidget.tsx` and the `components/index.ts` barrel aren't imported anywhere.

**26. Instagram view counts will go stale.**
They're hard-coded ("59.5K") with a comment to update them by hand. Either drop them or label them with a date.

Not covered: a code-comment pass (`antislop-code`). Ask if you want it.
